Clinical Excellence & Patient Trust

Dr. Lal Multi-Speciality Dental & Implant Clinic is a premier oral healthcare facility located in Shahdara, Delhi. Guided by 15+ years of clinical experience, our clinic provides world-class dental procedures under strict hygiene and sterilization standards.

Government Panel Empanelments

We are officially empaneled with major public sector healthcare schemes to offer cashless and subsidized treatments:

  • CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme)
  • DGHS (Directorate General of Health Services)
  • MCD (Municipal Corporation of Delhi)
  • DDA (Delhi Development Authority)
  • CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education)
