Dental Implants

Dental implants can be used as part of a tooth-replacement plan when clinically appropriate. Treatment starts with a detailed assessment and personalised planning.

01ConsultationUnderstand concerns and treatment goals.
02AssessmentClinical examination and imaging when indicated.
03PlanningDiscuss the appropriate restorative pathway.
04Follow-upReview healing and restoration as required.

Suitability depends on oral health, bone condition, medical history, treatment goals and a clinical examination. A dentist must determine whether implants are appropriate for an individual patient.

A typical pathway may involve consultation, assessment, treatment planning, implant placement when indicated, healing and restorative follow-up. The actual sequence varies according to the patient's clinical situation.
